Elevo Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

1. Who we are

ELEVO Property Management LLC ("Elevo") provides the Elevo app. This policy takes effect on November 21, 2026. To ask about your information, email hello@getelevo.app.

2. Information we collect

Account information: name, email, phone, and role (landlord, tenant, or vendor). A vendor can also enter a company name, service area, and license. Your password is handled by the sign-in service. We do not see the password itself. Property and unit information: the address, city, Arizona as the state, rent, deposits recorded as amounts, lease dates, and the tenant name and email when a landlord invites them. Messages, including one-to-one messages and group repair chats. Photos and documents you upload, including repair photos, leases, invoices, and files in the landlord private vault. Rent records: the payment methods a landlord lists, and the record created when someone marks rent as paid or confirms it. Elevo does not collect the rent money or the deposit. Screening: the applicant details a landlord enters, and the landlord's own notes. Elevo does not receive the RentSpree credit report. One Firebase Cloud Messaging device token per person, stored in push_subscriptions, so Elevo can send a push notification. Notification settings cover push and email. Elevo does not send text messages. Subscription information: the plan name and the trial end date saved on the account. Card numbers are typed on Stripe's pages, not in Elevo.

3. Who handles the information

Supabase stores the database, the files, and the sign-in accounts. Stripe handles landlord and vendor subscription checkout. Elevo opens Stripe's payment page and does not store the card number. Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications. Resend sends email, such as a welcome message or a tenant invitation, through a function on Supabase. RentSpree handles screening on its own site. Elevo is not affiliated with RentSpree and does not receive the credit report. We do not sell personal information. We do not show ads.

4. Who can see it

A landlord can see their own properties, units, tenants, messages for those units, documents, private vault, rent records, and repairs. A tenant can see the unit they currently rent, the landlord's payment instructions, their messages, their repairs, and documents the landlord shared for that tenancy. After the tenant moves out, they no longer see new messages, new files, or new repairs for that unit. A vendor can see repairs assigned to them and the repair chat while they are assigned. If the landlord assigns someone else, the first vendor can still see messages from the time they were on the job, not messages from after they left. The private vault is visible to that landlord. Tenants do not see it. People who operate Elevo can access the database to run and support the service. We do not use that access to sell your information.

5. Children

Elevo is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Sign-up does not ask for a birthday. If you believe a child has an account, email hello@getelevo.app and we will delete it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep information while the account is open. There is no automatic deletion date. Landlords, tenants, and vendors can delete an account in the app. After a confirmation, Elevo turns off that login and clears the name, email, and phone. The account row stays, so a property, unit, or payment instruction is not removed. Elevo also deletes notification settings, the Firebase device-token row, emergency contacts, and the private vault, and it removes storage files that are only theirs. Records other people still need are kept. That includes properties, units, rent instructions, repairs, documents, photo logs, and messages. Your name is removed from a tenancy, a repair you filed, a payment record, and message headers where the app can do that. The message text stays for the other people in the conversation. A landlord's payment instructions, including bank details they typed for tenants, stay so current tenants can still pay. Stripe webhook audit rows are kept. Deleting the account cancels the Stripe subscription immediately. There is no refund for the current period. You can also email hello@getelevo.app and ask us to delete the account or send a copy of what we hold.

7. A copy of your information

There is no button in the app to download everything we hold. Email hello@getelevo.app and ask for a copy. We will send what we have for your account.

8. Security

Sign-in is provided by Supabase. Repair photos and other private files are stored in private storage and opened with a short-lived link, not a public web address. Access rules limit each person to their own properties, tenancy, or assigned jobs. No storage method is perfect. We do not promise that Elevo cannot be broken into. Two-factor sign-in is not available.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change in the app and on the public privacy page. Email hello@getelevo.app with questions.
